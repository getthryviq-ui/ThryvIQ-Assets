📖 Research Glossary & Terminology

Golden Handcuffs (Structural Trap) A state where professional optionality is systematically diminished by the alignment of deferred compensation (RSUs/Options) with expanding fixed lifestyle costs.
Lifestyle Rigidity The percentage of base salary (excluding bonuses) consumed by non-negotiable fixed expenses. High rigidity creates a state of "Professional Captivity."
Allostatic Load The physiological "wear and tear" on the body caused by chronic stress. In the research, this is used to quantify the physical cost of staying in a high-burnout role.
Portable Capital Assets, skills, and professional networks that remain fully functional and valuable regardless of your current employment status.
Windfall Capture Strategy A prevention mechanism where 100% of any income exceeding base salary (bonuses, stock vests) is diverted to liquid autonomy-building assets.
Freedom-Adjusted Compensation A valuation model that discounts total compensation based on risk, vesting delays, and the "burnout penalty" required to earn it.
