How to Ensure Extras Go to Principal (Servicer Script) ▼
  • Direct Channel Setup: Set up automatic overpayments directly through the servicer's online portal to avoid third-party transfer fees.
  • Payment Coding: Select the "Principal Only" or "Curtailment" option online.
  • Check Memo Audit: If mailing a check, write "Apply extra funds to principal balance only. Do not advance due date."
  • Statement Reconciliation: Check your monthly statement to ensure the "Suspense/Unapplied Balance" is exactly $0.00.

Copy-Ready Email/Message to Servicer:

Subject: Principal Curtailment Instruction - Loan #[LOAN_NUMBER] To the Servicing Department, I am initiating additional payments on my mortgage (Loan #[LOAN_NUMBER]). Please code all funds received in excess of my standard contractual Monthly Periodic Payment exclusively as "Principal Only" / Principal Curtailment. Under no circumstances should these excess funds be placed into a suspense/unapplied account, nor should they be used to advance my next scheduled payment due date. Please apply the excess immediately to reduce my Unpaid Principal Balance (UPB). Thank you, [Your Name]
